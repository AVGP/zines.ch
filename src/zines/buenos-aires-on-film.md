Martin reminisces on his trip to Buenos Aires and how his perception changed as he walked through the streets.
From modern downtown to the colorful murals in the outskirts of the city, Martin explored the history and variety of expression
in Buenos Aires artists' home turf.

The photos were taken on a Nikon F70 camera with Harman Phoenix film, giving them a unique, warm look.
Martin rasterised the photos and broke them into four individual CMYK channels, printing two colors in two passes through a Riso M970.
