## What is a zine library?

A zine is a small, self-published magazine. It might be photocopied, stapled, riso-printed or sewn by hand. It's made because someone had something to say, not because it had to sell.

A zine library collects these little publications and makes them easy to find and read — like a public library, but for things you'd never find in a bookshop.

</div>
<div>

## Swiss at heart, open to everyone

Our focus is on zines made in Switzerland — from Basel to Lugano and Geneva to St. Gallen, in any language.

But a good zine is a good zine, so we happily shelve work from anywhere in the world. If you've printed something small and personal, there's a spot for it here.
